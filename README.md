# Tri-Vision ClearTrace

An AI governance platform that keeps a register of AI systems, checks each one against Canadian federal rules (and later NIST, ISO and EU frameworks), gives a clear five-state result with evidence and a certainty index for every control, lets people review and override with a tamper-evident audit trail, and watches data, model and output risk. It runs on-premises or in Canadian cloud, in English and French, for many organizations at once.

Status: under construction. The design is in [docs/blueprint/BLUEPRINT.md](docs/blueprint/BLUEPRINT.md). Requirement-to-test links are in [docs/blueprint/traceability.md](docs/blueprint/traceability.md).
